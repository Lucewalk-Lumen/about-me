# 审稿模板

本文档为可复用语句模板，供 审稿路径生成审稿意见时查阅。模板中的 `[...]` 需替换为稿件实际内容，不要照搬原文。

**使用规则**：
- 写中文审稿意见时，只使用中文模板
- 写英文审稿意见时，只使用英文模板
- 不要在同一段落中混用中英文模板

## （一）开头模板

### 中文开头（通用）

> 本文以[理论框架]为视角，采用[方法/样本]检验[核心关系]。该选题回应了[文献领域]中关于[具体问题]的讨论，在[数据/情境/方法]方面具有一定基础。然而，我认为当前稿件在[维度]方面仍存在若干需要进一步论证的问题，期待作者在修改中予以充分回应。

### 英文开头（通用）

> Drawing on [theoretical framework], this manuscript examines [core relationship] using [method/sample]. The topic speaks to ongoing conversations in [literature domain] regarding [specific issue], and the study has some strengths in [data/context/method]. That said, I have several concerns regarding [dimension] that I believe warrant further attention in a revision.

### 中文开头（肯定型）

> 本文聚焦[问题]，以[理论]为框架，采用[方法]探讨[关系]。选题具有[理论/实践]价值，研究设计[优点]，数据分析[优点]。以下意见供作者参考，以期进一步提升稿件质量。

### 英文开头（肯定型）

> This manuscript addresses [issue] through the lens of [theory], using [method] to examine [relationship]. The topic is of [theoretical/practical] significance, and the research design [strength] and data analysis [strength] provide a reasonable foundation. My comments below are intended to help the authors further strengthen the manuscript.

### 中文开头（问题型）

> 本文试图以[理论]为框架探讨[问题]，但在理论贡献的深度、研究设计的严谨性和结果解释的准确性方面存在若干需要实质性修改的问题。以下按维度展开具体意见。

### 中文开头（人类审稿口吻·先肯定后批评）

> 本研究基于[理论/视角]，从[角度]探究了[核心问题]。[概括研究内容与设计]。首先，弥补了现有研究[缺口]的不足；其次，扩展了[领域]的[变量/机制]认识；最后，研究内容比较贴合实际，为[实践对象]提供了理论依据。然而本研究仍存在很多的不足之处，建议根据以下意见进行大修。

> 例（样本实写）：本研究基于"特质-心理状态-行为"模型，从个体人格特质视角探究了黑暗人格如何影响知识共享敌意的产生……首先，弥补了现有理论与实证研究只关注组织内知识共享敌意的产生机制，而未对个体层面加以讨论的不足……然而本研究仍存在很多的不足之处，建议根据以下意见进行大修。

### 英文开头（人类审稿口吻·先肯定后批评）

> This study draws on [theory] to examine how and when [IV] may prompt [mechanism], and [moderator] in turn inspires [DV]. The authors collect survey data from [sample] to examine their hypotheses and find support for most of their arguments. I think the authors have examined an important and interesting topic. As I admire their effort in conducting this research, I also think there are a few points they need to consider before this manuscript can be published.

> 例（样本实写）：This study draws on the interactionist model of creativity to examine how and when green entrepreneurial orientation may prompt a person to strengthen psychological green climate... I think the authors have examined an important and interesting topic. As I admire their effort in conducting this research, I also think there are a few points they need to consider before this manuscript can be published.

### 结尾（人类审稿口吻·礼貌收尾）

> 中文：希望上述意见对作者修改稿件有所帮助，祝修改顺利。
> 英文：I hope the authors may find my suggestions useful in revising the manuscript, and I wish them well with the revision.

### 英文开头（问题型）

> The manuscript seeks to examine [issue] through [theory], but there are several substantive concerns regarding the depth of theoretical contribution, the rigor of the research design, and the interpretation of findings that require significant revision. I elaborate on these concerns below, organized by dimension.

## （二）理论贡献模板

### 原创性不足（中文）

> 我认为稿件尚未充分说明研究的原创性。目前的论证主要强调[作者当前说法]，但这并不足以证明本文带来了[新机制/新边界条件/新情境]。更关键的是，作者需要解释[应补充的理论张力或缺口]。建议作者在引言部分进一步澄清[具体修改方向]。

### 原创性不足（英文）

> I do not think the manuscript has sufficiently established its originality. The current argument mainly emphasizes [authors' current claim], but this does not yet demonstrate that the paper offers [new mechanism/new boundary condition/new context]. More importantly, the authors need to explain [missing theoretical tension or gap]. I encourage the authors to clarify [specific revision direction] in the introduction.

### 贡献停留在边缘延伸（中文）

> 目前稿件将贡献主要建立在"已有研究较少"之上，但尚未充分说明这一缺口为何具有理论后果。作者需要更清楚地说明本文究竟改变了哪个文献对话，以及没有本文时既有理论解释会在哪里不足。目前的新意更接近情境性延伸，而非理论机制上的推进。

### 贡献停留在边缘延伸（英文）

> The manuscript currently motivates its contribution largely by noting that the topic has received limited attention, but it does not yet explain why this gap is theoretically consequential. The authors need to clarify which scholarly conversation the manuscript seeks to change and where existing theory remains insufficient without the present study. The novelty appears closer to a contextual extension than to a substantive theoretical advancement.

### 文献定位不清（中文）

> 稿件的文献定位不够清晰。作者提到[引用的文献或理论]，但未充分说明本文与[核心文献群]的关系。建议作者明确进入哪个文献对话，以及本文相较于既有研究的独特贡献。

### 文献定位不清（英文）

> The manuscript's positioning in the literature is unclear. The authors mention [cited literature or theory], but do not sufficiently explain how this paper relates to [core literature stream]. I suggest the authors clarify which conversation they are entering and what unique contribution this paper makes relative to existing research.

### 理论标签化（中文）

> 稿件引用了[理论名称]，但该理论并未真正驱动研究假设。当前理论框架更像是模型的标签，而不是生成假设的解释机制。建议作者深入阐述[理论]的核心逻辑，并说明为什么该理论是解释[现象]的最佳选择。

### 理论标签化（英文）

> The manuscript cites [theory name], but this theory does not truly drive the hypotheses. The theoretical framework currently functions more as a label for the model than as the mechanism that generates the hypotheses. I encourage the authors to elaborate on the core logic of [theory] and explain why it is the best lens for understanding [phenomenon].

### 假设机制不清（中文）

> 假设[编号]预测了[关系]，但未充分说明其背后的机制。作者需要解释[自变量]通过什么途径影响[因变量]，以及为什么该机制是可信的。假设推导需要更明确地解释过程机制，而不仅是引用既有相关研究。建议作者补充理论推理，明确中介机制或边界条件。

### 假设机制不清（英文）

> Hypothesis [number] predicts [relationship], but the underlying mechanism is not sufficiently explained. The hypothesis development should more clearly articulate the process through which [independent variable] affects [dependent variable], rather than relying primarily on prior associations. I suggest the authors supplement their theoretical reasoning by specifying the mediating mechanism or boundary conditions.

### 构念混淆（中文）

> 稿件中[构念A]与[构念B]的边界不够清晰。两者在[定义/测量/理论功能]上存在重叠，可能导致读者混淆。建议作者明确区分这两个构念，并说明选择[构念A]而非[构念B]的理由。

### 构念混淆（英文）

> The boundary between [construct A] and [construct B] is unclear in the manuscript. There is overlap in [definition/measurement/theoretical function], which may confuse readers. I suggest the authors clearly distinguish these two constructs and explain why [construct A] rather than [construct B] was chosen.

### 理论贡献薄弱（中文）

> 稿件的理论贡献需要进一步强化。目前的贡献主要体现在[情境延伸/样本拓展]，但这不足以支撑在[期刊名称]发表。建议作者思考：如果没有这篇论文，文献对该现象的理解是否仍然完整？本文能否指出既有理论无法解释的矛盾？

### 理论贡献薄弱（英文）

> The theoretical contribution of the manuscript needs to be strengthened. The current contribution mainly lies in [context extension/sample expansion], which may not be sufficient for publication in [journal name]. I encourage the authors to consider: Would the literature's understanding of this phenomenon remain complete without this paper? Can this paper identify contradictions that existing theories cannot explain?

### 变量选择不连贯（中文）

> 作者需要说明为什么这些变量共同构成一个连贯的理论模型，而不是若干关系的拼接。如果删掉[变量名称]，模型的理论故事是否仍然成立？建议作者解释变量选择的统一理论逻辑。

### 变量选择不连贯（英文）

> The authors need to explain why these variables jointly constitute a coherent theoretical model rather than a set of loosely connected relationships. Would the theoretical story of the model still hold if [variable name] were removed? I suggest the authors explain the unified theoretical logic behind their variable selection.

### 层级错配（中文）

> 理论层级、测量层级和分析层级之间仍需进一步对齐。稿件在理论部分讨论的是[层级]现象，但测量和分析却停留在[层级]层面，这可能削弱结论的可信度。

### 层级错配（英文）

> The level of theory, level of measurement, and level of analysis need to be more carefully aligned. The manuscript discusses [level] phenomena in the theory section, but the measurement and analysis remain at the [level] level, which may weaken the credibility of the conclusions.

### 替代理论未处理（中文）

> 稿件未充分处理竞争性理论解释。[替代理论]可能同样能解释[核心关系]，但作者未说明当前理论为何优于替代理论。建议作者讨论理论边界和竞争性解释。

### 替代理论未处理（英文）

> The manuscript does not sufficiently address competing theoretical explanations. [Alternative theory] could also explain [core relationship], but the authors do not explain why the current theory is superior. I suggest the authors discuss theoretical boundaries and competing explanations.

### 理论基础展开不足（中文）

> 理论框架部分的展开尚不充分。当前对[理论名称]的阐述仅停留在提及理论名称和提出者的层面，未深入展开其核心逻辑——包括该理论的关键构念、构念间的理论关系、以及理论适用的边界条件。对于一篇以[理论名称]为核心框架的实证研究而言，读者需要理解该理论如何从一般性命题转化为本研究的特定预测。建议作者将理论框架扩展为独立章节，系统阐述[理论]的核心逻辑，并明确说明该理论如何为每条假设提供推导基础。

### 理论基础展开不足（英文）

> The theoretical framework section would benefit from more thorough elaboration. The current treatment of [theory name] remains at the level of naming the theory and its originator, without unpacking its core logic—including the key constructs, their theoretical interrelationships, and the boundary conditions under which the theory operates. For an empirical study that positions [theory name] as its central framework, the reader needs to understand how the theory's general propositions translate into the specific predictions of this study. I encourage the authors to expand the theoretical framework into a dedicated section that systematically elaborates the core logic of [theory] and clarifies how it informs each hypothesis.

### 假设推导缺乏理论起点（中文）

> 假设[编号]的推导以"已有研究发现"为起点，而非从[理论名称]的核心逻辑出发。当前推导主要依赖既有实证研究的堆砌——依次引用若干研究发现X与Y相关的文献，然后直接跳到假设陈述。这种推导方式未能体现理论驱动研究的逻辑：为什么[理论]的核心命题意味着X应当影响Y？该理论提供了怎样的机制解释？建议作者重构假设推导，以理论逻辑为起点，将既有实证作为辅助证据而非主要论据。

### 假设推导缺乏理论起点（英文）

> The development of Hypothesis [number] begins with prior empirical findings rather than with the core logic of [theory name]. The current derivation relies primarily on stacking empirical precedents—citing studies that have found associations between X and Y, then proceeding directly to the hypothesis statement. This approach does not reflect theory-driven reasoning: why would the core propositions of [theory] imply that X should influence Y? What mechanism does the theory provide? I suggest the authors restructure the hypothesis development to begin with theoretical logic, using prior empirical findings as supporting rather than primary evidence.

### 中介假设机制链断裂（中文）
